# Example HR Support Runbook — Fictional Demo Procedures

This runbook is fictional demo content, not an approved company process. Answers based on it must say they describe example procedures.

## HR Request Routing
For leave and attendance policy questions, consult the HR handbook. For a personal leave balance, contact HR through the employee portal. For pay discrepancies, use the private payroll support channel. For benefits eligibility, contact the benefits team through HR. Do not invent contact addresses or promise resolution times.

## Employee Information Changes
Under this example procedure, employees update permitted contact information through the secure HR portal. Changes to bank details, identity documents, or tax information must use approved secure channels and any required verification. The copilot cannot make changes or verify identity.

## Offboarding
Under this example procedure, contact HR for the approved resignation and offboarding process. Confirm notice requirements using your employment agreement and applicable HR policy. Coordinate handover with your manager and follow HR instructions for returning company property. Final pay and benefits depend on individual terms and applicable rules; HR must confirm them.

## Performance Reviews
Under this example procedure, employees consult the review schedule and role expectations in the HR portal, prepare a self-assessment, and discuss goals with their manager. Contact HR for process questions. Do not infer ratings, promotions, or compensation outcomes.

## Missing or Conflicting Policies
If evidence is missing, outdated, or conflicting, explain the limitation and refer to HR for the current approved document. Public web guidance does not establish internal company policy. Do not provide employment-law conclusions or disclose other employees' information.
