# Example HR Handbook — Fictional Demo Policies

These are fictional demonstration policies for NovaRetail, not actual company policies or legal guidance. Replace this document with approved HR policies before production use. Every answer using this document must identify it as an example.

## Annual Leave
Under this fictional example policy, submit annual leave requests through the employee HR portal with the requested dates and leave type. Check your available balance in the portal. Your manager reviews the request; submitting a request does not approve it. If your balance appears incorrect or the portal is unavailable, contact HR. Entitlements depend on your applicable employment terms; this handbook does not establish a number of leave days.

## Sick Leave
Under this fictional example policy, notify your manager as soon as reasonably possible and record your absence in the HR portal. Ask HR whether documentation is required under the applicable policy. Do not share medical details in a public channel or with this copilot.

## Benefits
Under this fictional example policy, review the benefits guide in the HR portal for available plans and enrollment instructions. Eligibility, coverage, and enrollment windows depend on the approved plan documents and employment terms. Contact HR for individual eligibility or a qualifying life event. The copilot cannot access your enrollment records.

## Payroll Questions
Under this fictional example policy, review your payslip and pay period in the HR portal. Report a suspected discrepancy using the private payroll support channel linked in the portal. Include the affected pay period and a brief description. Do not paste bank details, tax identifiers, salary statements, or other personal payroll records into the copilot. Payroll must investigate and confirm any correction.

## Onboarding
Under this fictional example policy, new employees review their onboarding checklist in the HR portal, complete required forms through approved secure channels, read the employee handbook, and arrange an introduction with their manager. HR confirms which documents and training apply to the employee's role and location.

## Workplace Concerns
Under this fictional example policy, use the confidential HR reporting channel for concerns about workplace conduct, discrimination, or harassment. If the concern involves your manager, contact HR directly. Do not name other employees or disclose detailed allegations in the copilot. HR will explain the reporting process; the copilot cannot investigate or determine an outcome.
